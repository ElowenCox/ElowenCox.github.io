# Portfolio Rubric

Total: 30 points, six categories worth 5 points each.

## 1. Overall look and feel — 5

Full marks require:
- Wonderful first impression
- Name and short description immediately visible
- Obvious navigation
- Attractive presentation
- Accessibility compliance
- Clear purpose

## 2. Bio — 5

Full marks require:
- Interesting and professional background/experience
- Clear, well-written prose
- School
- Location
- Graduation date or degree
- No typos
- A compelling sense of the person

## 3. Research — 5

Full marks require:
- Clear research statement
- Minimal jargon / understandable to non-specialists
- Research sounds interesting and purposeful
- Journal articles and presentations cited
- DOI links and article links when applicable

No publication may be invented. Missing DOI/link information must remain missing until supplied.

## 4. Teaching — 5

Full marks require:
- Clear teaching statement
- Teaching philosophy
- Teaching experience
- Strong sense of educational commitment

Because the supplied CV does not document conventional academic teaching positions, use the broader and accurate framing:
**Teaching, Training & Mentoring**

## 5. Personal information — 5

Full marks require:
- Interesting and professional personal interests
- Clear writing
- Fun facts
- Human dimension
- No typos

## 6. CV — 5

Full marks require:
- CV viewable on the web
- CV downloadable
- CV easy to find

5. Do not describe the 2026 Glasgow presentation as completed; it is pending/upcoming.
6. Do not characterize Elowen as a conventional academic faculty member.
7. Distinguish research mentoring, professional training, military training, and academic teaching.
8. Prefer specific evidence over adjectives.
9. Keep content modular so publications, presentations, research projects, and CV versions can be added later.
10. Do not remove working rubric requirements merely to simplify the design.
11. After substantive changes, run the QA checklist and perform a rubric audit.
12. If required information is missing, ask for it rather than inventing it.

## Editing workflow

ANALYZE -> PLAN -> IMPLEMENT -> ACCESSIBILITY QA -> RESPONSIVE QA -> RUBRIC AUDIT -> REVISE -> FINAL AUDIT

## Design principle

The site should feel like the portfolio of a technically rigorous engineer who also has a strong scientific and creative identity—not a generic corporate website and not a conventional academic CV site.
